OffMeta — Natural Language Magic Card Search

OffMeta is a natural language Magic: The Gathering card search tool. It lets players describe cards the way they talk and translates that into real Scryfall searches. No syntax knowledge required. Type something like "cheap green ramp spells", "creatures that make treasure tokens" or "budget alternatives to Rhystic Study" and OffMeta builds the Scryfall query for you, shows it, and lets you edit it.
