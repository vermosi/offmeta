# Product Audits Archive

This archive keeps older iterative audit notes that informed the current roadmap and search positioning.
They are retained here for historical context, not as active planning documents.

## Search Experience Audit

The search experience audit identified the main issues that shaped the current search-focused roadmap:

- homepage copy leaned too generic instead of clearly grounding the product in plain-English search
- the first screen had too many competing actions
- example queries needed tighter curation around the strongest supported searches
- result-state next steps were too split and repetitive
- the product story was spread across too many sections instead of being shown in one clear path

The resulting changes focused on:

- repositioning the hero around plain-English search
- removing extra hero links so search stays primary
- curating example queries around supported search intents
- tightening the Scryfall comparison copy
- reducing competing next-step actions after results

## Product Audit

The product audit captured the broader iterative plan that followed the search experience audit.
The main takeaways were:

- the product needed stronger positioning as a discovery tool, not just a translator
- result views needed clearer next-step affordances
- the homepage and results flow needed stronger contrast with Scryfall
- query sharing and first-use guidance were important supporting behaviors
- mobile scanability and result-card hierarchy were worth tightening

That work has since moved into the roadmap and implementation backlog, so this archive remains only as background context.
